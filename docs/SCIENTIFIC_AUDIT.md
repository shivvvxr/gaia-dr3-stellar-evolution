# Scientific audit notes

This repository audit was limited to internal consistency, reproducibility, and the scientific scope stated by the manuscript.

## Supported claims

- The final sample is 1,399 stars from a 9,974-row matched catalogue.
- The global stage-mass Spearman association is negative and statistically strong.
- The sign survives the reported astrometric, distance, magnitude, and mass-range sensitivity checks.
- The mass distribution is heterogeneous, with BIC favoring three components among k=1,2,3; the two-component fit is descriptive.
- The lower-mass component becomes much more prevalent at high evolutionary stage in the selected catalogue.

## Claims explicitly rejected by the paper

- The early-late median difference is not an individual-star mass-loss measurement.
- The permutation test is not a physical no-mass-loss model.
- The GMM components are not asserted to be two physical populations.
- The high-mass component is not treated as independently validated 2.830-solar-mass stars.

## Important limitations

The analysis is cross-sectional, model-derived, and magnitude limited; no full binary correction or synthetic physical no-mass-loss population is constructed; direct inverse-parallax distance is used only as a sensitivity diagnostic; metallicity control does not remove FLAME model dependence.

## External reference check

The manuscript's descriptions of FLAME giant-star flags, published mass percentiles, and the approximate 1-2-solar-mass caution range were checked against Gaia DR3 documentation during the audit.
